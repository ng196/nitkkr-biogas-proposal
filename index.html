1. Executive summary

NITKKR plans a small on-campus anaerobic digestion (biogas) pilot that turns segregated non-hostel wet waste and STP sludge into cooking gas and digestate manure.

  • Campus solid waste is 1,615,027 kg/yr measured (Deswal/Prasad 2024), about ≈4,425 kg/d estimate (÷365; total campus waste, not a digester feed).
  • Usable streams in scope: residential colony wet waste, horticulture, commercial canteen, and STP waste-activated sludge. Only colony and sludge have bands so far; horticulture and canteen masses are empty.
  • Gas output, digester size and cost are empty until stream masses and TS/VS/BMP are fixed.
  • Central subsidy (MNRE CFA) is not banked: NBP Phase-I ended 30.09.2026 measured. Plan assumes institute funding.
  • Regulatory aim: HSPCB White category (no discharge, digestate used as manure) — confirmation empty.
