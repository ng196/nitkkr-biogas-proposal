1. What this proposal is about

NITKKR is preparing a Pre-DPR for a small on-campus anaerobic digestion (biogas) pilot that would process segregated non-hostel wet waste and STP sludge into cooking gas and digestate manure.

  • This page is the living outline. Module shells stay empty until accepted NIT campus measurements (or locked institute decisions) land.
  • Sample / reference proposals are listed under Refs for structure only — they are not our numbers.
  • Methodology for gas, sizing, and cost is TBD until accepted NIT refs lock.
