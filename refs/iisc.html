Design aspects :

   1. IISc is interested in a multi-feed biogas plant for its solid wastes that comprises of MSW
      collected from residential units, eateries, street bins, garden litter and occasionally leaf litter
      collected within the campus. The biomethanation technology and plant design should be
      capable to use any of these as feedstock totalling up to 2 tons per day (tpd).

   2. IISc has initiated a door to door segregated dry-wet waste collection system. Segregation is
      likely to be less than perfect. The biomethanation technology should be capable of accepting
      small deviations from ideal 100% segregation without too much down time caused by it. IISc
      will attempt a zero waste campus and so it is desirable that all wet wastes be fed to the
      biomethanation plant without need for secondary segregation or pre-processing such as
      grinding, pulverizing, size reduction, etc. It is desirable to use the 3-zone horizontal plug flow
      technology.

   3. IISc looks forward to convert its leaf litter to compost for generation of compost for internal
      uses. The plant layout and design should be amenable for expansion to use leaf litter as
      feedstock or switch to this feedstock without need for design or process changes.

   4. A biogas plant should preferably be built from non-corrosive materials with a life of 20years. A
      low or no-maintenance biomethanation process /design would be preferred in order to
      demonstrate its capability to other campuses to adopt a similar waste management system.

   5. It is preferred that to meet the above SWM policy and strategy needs, a horizontal plug flow
      reactor (PFR) technology be put in place. Technology and design to be chosen should have
      worked satisfactorily for at least 10 years in public places. The reactor section of the PFR should
      either be built from random stone or burnt brick masonry with pilasters while the roof be built
      with corrosion-resistant (for hydrogen sulphide /acetic acid) bricks. Use of RCC, RCC beams
      should be kept to a minimum and should always located external to the reactor. Direct
      exposure of RCC beams biogas and digester slurry needs to be avoided or kept to a bare
      minimum. A masonry vault roof is suggested for the biomethanation plant. Among the
      available PFR designs, the ASTRA-PFR designs would be preferred because of its ruggedness and
      robustness as well for having been proven in the field and absence for the need for pre-
      processing.

                                                                                                pg. 39
6. A separate gas storage unit is preferred. The gas storage system should be unaffected by and
   protected against inclement weather, insects, rodents and birds and should work at moderate
   gas pressures. The storage capacity should be at least 70% of projected daily gas production
   levels and excess gas, if any should be flared. All these should form the gas collection and
   storage unit. It is desirable to provide for a flame-proof biogas blower capable of compressing
   up to 150cm water pressure for possible use as LPG substitute later on.

7. The lay out should allow direct feeding of collected MSW and other wastes with minimum of
   human handling. A mechanical feeding option would be desirable. The layout of the inlet needs
   to allow basic inspection of received MSW, spread and extract any undesirables and rapid
   feeding to this spot. The design needs to permit washing /cleaning of the inlet systems
   immediately after completion of the daily feeding operation as a means to avoid flies and poor
   aesthetics.

8. The outlet system should include removal of digester liquid, extraction and removal of digested
   feedstock residues and covering these outlets after digested material extraction. The outlet
   coverings should be safe for operators to step upon in case of need. The technology service
   provider should also make provision of easy maintenance of the inside of the reactors as need
